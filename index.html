How should I interpret the results?
  • Compare the selected portfolio's range against the AI scale you are studying.
  • Ask whether capacity is dispatchable or intermittent and whether the location can support the technology.
  • Cost ranges represent approximate total project capital costs from the supplied course workbook, not lifetime costs.
  • Project timelines may overlap; the workbook uses the longest selected technology as a simplified portfolio window.
